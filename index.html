Suunto / Product & agent experience

Suunto AI Coach

A context-aware sports & health agent. From daily signals to practical next steps.

Role
Product Manager / AI Product Manager
Form
Recorded prototype, about 84 seconds

User need

Fragmented sleep, training and recovery context.

Product value

Conversational insights and practical next steps.

What this is. The film is a product recording, not a clinical system, a public launch record, or proof that every backend tool completed its call. My work as Product Manager / AI Product Manager was the reasoning around the path: discovery and scoping, intent routing, prompts and tools, and evaluation design with engineers. It is not sole full-stack authorship, and it is not a product-lead claim.

From context to next steps.

Interpretation & boundaries. Context shapes suggestions. Missing tools are disclosed. Evaluate health guardrails.

  1. 01 Retrieve context

    A question about recent sleep brings back a summary from the prototype.

  2. 02 Compare plan & actual

    The middle still shows the actual training record. It is not a finished before-and-after.

  3. 03 Suggest a route

    The conversation offers closed-loop routes, including distance and climb.

  4. 04 Explain limits

    Later in the recording the assistant discloses a tool it does not have. A reminder on screen is not treated as proof that an outside tool succeeded.

Cross-Border Diagnostic

Next · HyperSpot

Civic AI / Concept prototype

HyperSpot

A conversational civic agent for issue intake, clarification and report drafting.

The intended workflow is the work. A person describes an infrastructure issue, the agent clarifies location and severity, then a report and routing guidance are drafted. That sequence is a concept. It is not a record of a submitted report, a government channel, or a working civic app.

  1. 01 / Describe An infrastructure issue.
  2. 02 / Clarify Location and severity.
  3. 03 / Draft Report and routing guidance.

hyperspot.icu is a public marketing showcase, not a live app or a demo of civic submission.
